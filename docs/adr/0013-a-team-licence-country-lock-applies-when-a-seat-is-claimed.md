# A Team Licence's Country Lock applies when a Seat is claimed, not afterwards

A Seat on a Team Licence bought at a Purchasing-Power Price can only be claimed from the country the Team Licence was bought in. Once claimed, the Seat is not checked again, so a Student on a Claimed Seat can take the course from anywhere, unlike a Student with an Individual Purchase. A team's members travel and work remotely, and the Team Admin has already chosen who gets each Seat; the claim is the moment the discount is used. A Team Licence bought at full price has no Country Lock at all, so its Seats can be claimed from any country.

## Considered Options

- **Lock every Seat to the buying country, whatever the price**: rejected, because a full-price Team Licence has no discount to protect, and it stopped a company's staff abroad from claiming Seats.
- **Check Claimed Seats on every visit, like an Individual Purchase**: rejected, because it would lock out team members who travel, for a discount their Team Admin already paid for.
