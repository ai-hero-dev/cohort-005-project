# Approving a Refund Request keeps the learner's progress

When a Platform Admin approves a Refund Request on an Individual Purchase, the buyer loses their place on the course but keeps their lesson progress, watch history, quiz attempts, comments and rating. Deleting it can't be undone, the watch history is the evidence the approval was decided on, and a learner who buys again or claims a Seat later should pick up where they left off. Rows for a learner with no place on the course are expected, not orphans to clean up.
